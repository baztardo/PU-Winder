# 🚨 CRITICAL BUG FIXED - v1.8.3!

## THE PROBLEM:
Your config.h changes were **IGNORED**!

```cpp
main.cpp line 257:
params.spindle_rpm = 60.0f;  // ❌ HARDCODED!
```

**It was NOT reading WINDING_SPINDLE_RPM from config.h!**

---

## WHAT WAS HAPPENING:

| Your Config | Code Used | Encoder | Why? |
|-------------|-----------|---------|------|
| 60.0f | 60.0f | 30 RPM | Hardcoded 60! |
| 120.0f | 60.0f | 30 RPM | Hardcoded 60! |
| 240.0f | 60.0f | 30 RPM | Hardcoded 60! |

No matter what you changed in config.h, it always ran at 60 RPM stepper!

With ratio 2.0:
- Stepper 60 RPM → Spindle 30 RPM (encoder reading)

---

## v1.8.3 FIX:

```cpp
// OLD (hardcoded):
params.spindle_rpm = 60.0f;

// NEW (reads config.h):
params.spindle_rpm = WINDING_SPINDLE_RPM;
```

Now ALL parameters read from config.h:
- ✅ target_turns
- ✅ spindle_rpm
- ✅ wire_diameter_mm
- ✅ layer_width_mm
- ✅ start_position_mm
- ✅ ramp_time_sec
