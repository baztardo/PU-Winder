### Start Low and Ramp Up:

1. **480 config** - Should work smoothly ✅
2. **600 config** - 300 stepper / 600 spindle (old limit)
3. **840 config** - 420 stepper / 840 spindle (your previous test)
4. **1000 config** - 500 stepper / 1000 spindle
5. **1200 config** - 600 stepper / 1200 spindle (NEW LIMIT!)

### Watch For:

**✅ Good signs:**
- Smooth acceleration
- Stable RPM during winding
- Encoder counts match turns
- Motor sounds normal

**⚠️ Warning signs:**
- Motor makes grinding/squealing noise
- RPM oscillates/unstable
- Encoder freezes/glitches
- Turn count errors
- Motor gets hot (>60°C)

**❌ Failure modes:**
- Motor stalls (stops turning)
- Skipped steps (turn count way off)
- System crashes (OOM, watchdog)
- Motor overheats (>80°C)
