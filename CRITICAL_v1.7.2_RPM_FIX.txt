============================================
ROOT CAUSE:
============================================

The TMC2209 is **physically configured for 4x microstepping**
via the MS1/MS2 pins on the SKR Pico board!

UART commands CAN'T override this hardware configuration!

Firmware assumed: 8x = 1600 steps/rev
Hardware reality: 4x = 800 steps/rev

Result: Everything runs 2x faster!

============================================
THE MATH PROOF:
============================================

v1.7.1 (WRONG):
  Target: 60 RPM
  Command: 1600 sps (assuming 8x = 1600 steps/rev)
  Reality: 1600 sps ÷ 800 steps/rev (4x) = 2.0 RPS = 120 RPM ✅

v1.7.2 (CORRECT):
  Target: 60 RPM  
  Command: 800 sps (correctly using 4x = 800 steps/rev)
  Reality: 800 sps ÷ 800 steps/rev (4x) = 1.0 RPS = 60 RPM ✅
