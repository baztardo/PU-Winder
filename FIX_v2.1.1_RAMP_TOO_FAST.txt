# 🔧 v2.1.1 - FIX: Ramp-Up Too Aggressive!

## THE PROBLEM (v2.1.0):

```
1200 config: ✅ Works (tach 600 RPM)
1400 config: ⚠️ Inconsistent!
  - Sometimes stalls @ 300-400 RPM
  - Sometimes reaches 700 RPM  
  - "Depends on how smooth the start is"
```

**This is NOT a speed limit!**  
**This is an ACCELERATION problem!**

---

## ROOT CAUSE:

**Ramp-up is too fast!**

```
OLD: 0 → 1400 RPM in 5 seconds
Acceleration: 280 RPM/second

Like flooring the gas pedal!
Motor can't accelerate that fast!
Stalls during ramp-up!
```

**Once at speed, motor runs fine!**

---

## THE FIX (v2.1.1):

**Doubled ramp time:**

```c
OLD: #define WINDING_RAMP_TIME_SEC 5.0f   // Too aggressive!
NEW: #define WINDING_RAMP_TIME_SEC 10.0f  // Gentler acceleration
```

**New acceleration:**
```
0 → 1400 RPM in 10 seconds
140 RPM/second (half the rate!)
Much smoother!
```

---

## 📊 ACCELERATION COMPARISON:

| Ramp Time | Acceleration | Result |
|-----------|--------------|--------|
| **5 sec** | 280 RPM/s | ❌ Stalls @ 300-400 RPM |
| **10 sec** | 140 RPM/s | ⚠️ Should be smooth! |
| **15 sec** | 93 RPM/s | ✅ Very gentle |

## 💡 WHY THIS HAPPENS:

**Stepper motors have:**
- ✅ High torque at low speed
- ⚠️ Limited acceleration capability
- ✅ Good torque at high speed (once spinning!)

**Like a heavy flywheel:**
- Hard to start spinning quickly
- But easy to keep spinning once going!

**Solution:**
- Slow ramp-up (give motor time to accelerate)
- Once at speed, can maintain high RPM!
