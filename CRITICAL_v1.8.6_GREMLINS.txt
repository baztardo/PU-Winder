## BUG #3: ENCODER FREEZES AT HIGH SPEED ❌

Your 500 RPM test:
```
Ramp: rpm=57.0 ✓ (tracking during ramp)
Continuous: rpm=75.4 → 4.9 → 0.2 ❌ (frozen!)
Position stuck at ~7072
But motor still physically turning!
```

### Why Encoder Freezes:

1. **PIO FIFO Overflow**
   ```pio
   push noblock  ; If FIFO full, DISCARD!
   ```
   At high speed, CPU can't drain FIFO fast enough!

2. **Speed Too High for ISR**
   - Encoder ISR runs every 50μs (HEARTBEAT_US)
   - At 500 RPM target (really 13333 sps during ramp!):
     - That's ~10000 counts/sec
     - ISR needs to process ~0.5 counts per call
     - Might be overloading!

3. **Electrical Noise**
   - High motor current at high speed
   - Encoder signals getting noisy
