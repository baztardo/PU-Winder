## ⚠️ WHAT TO WATCH FOR:

### ✅ Good Signs:
- Motor runs smoothly
- No grinding/squealing noises
- Tach shows target RPM
- Motor doesn't overheat (<60°C)

### ⚠️ Warning Signs:
- Motor makes noise
- Speed unstable
- Motor warm (40-60°C)

### ❌ Failure Signs:
- Motor stalls (stops turning)
- Grinding/screeching sounds  
- Motor very hot (>80°C)
- System crashes

---

## 🔥 IF MOTOR STALLS:

**Need TMC2209 optimization:**
1. SpreadCycle mode (better for high RPM)
2. Current tuning (IRUN/IHOLD)
3. StealthChop → SpreadCycle transition
